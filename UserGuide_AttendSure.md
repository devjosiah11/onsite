# User Guide: AttendSure Application

Welcome to the **AttendSure** app. This application is designed for reliable, location-based attendance tracking.

---

## 👨‍🎓 For Students

### 1. Daily Dashboard
- **Step 1**: Log in to the app.
- **Step 2**: Your dashboard shows **"Today's Classes"** at the top. 
- **Step 3**: Sessions that are currently accepting attendance will be highlighted with a **"TAP TO SIGN IN"** button.

### 2. Sign-In Workflow
- **Step 1**: Tap on the active session you wish to join.
- **Step 2**: **Location Check**: The app will automatically acquire your GPS coordinates. You must be within the classroom geofence to proceed.
- **Step 3**: **Biometric Scan**: Complete the identity check using your phone's biometric hardware (FaceID/Fingerprint).
- **Step 4**: **Verification**:
    - **Camera Scan**: Use the built-in scanner to capture the lecturer's QR code.
    - **OTP Entry**: Alternatively, enter the 6-digit One Time Password (OTP) displayed by the lecturer.
- **Step 5**: Upon success, you will see a confirmation message and be redirected back to the dashboard.

### 3. Attendance History
- **Step 1**: Go to the **"History"** tab.
- **Step 2**: View your overall attendance percentage and detailed logs for each session.
- **Step 3**: Pull down to refresh if you've just signed in and want to see your updated status.

---

## 👨‍🏫 For Lecturers (Portal Mode)

### 1. Opening a Session
- **Step 1**: In the Lecturer Portal, tap the **"Open New Session"** floating button.
- **Step 2**: Select the course, set the attendance window (default 15 mins), and confirm the start time.
- **Step 3**: **Location Update**: If you are in a new classroom, use the **"Update Location (GPS)"** button to pin the classroom's coordinates so students can sign in accurately.

### 2. Displaying Codes
- **Step 1**: Tap **"Show QR"** on any active session card.
- **Step 2**: Present the QR code and OTP to your students.
- **Step 3**: The code refreshes every **60 seconds**, but students have a **2-minute grace period** to use older codes.

---

## 💡 Troubleshooting
- **"Outside Geofence"**: Ensure you are physically inside the classroom. If the error persists, ask the lecturer to use the "Update Location" tool to calibrate the classroom's center point.
- **"Device Mismatch"**: Your account is bound to your first signed-in device. Contact admin if you have changed your phone.
- **Refreshing**: Pull down on the dashboard to fetch the latest session status.
